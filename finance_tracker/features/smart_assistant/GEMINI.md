## Goal
Provide intelligent financial recommendations and insights to help users manage their finances better.

## Features to Build

### 1. Spending Analysis
Analyze transaction data to identify:
- Top spending categories.
- Potentially unusual or high spending in certain categories.

### 2. Savings Suggestions
Based on spending analysis, suggest areas where the user could potentially save money.
- Example: "You spent a lot on 'Food' this month. Consider packing lunch twice a week to save."

### 3. Budget Optimization
Suggest adjustments to existing budgets based on actual spending patterns.
- Example: "Your 'Entertainment' budget is consistently underspent. You could reallocate some of it to 'Savings'."

### 4. Financial Tips
Offer general, relevant financial tips based on the user's overall financial health (e.g., if they are consistently over budget, suggest reviewing expenses).

## Success Criteria
✅ Can identify top spending categories.
✅ Can detect high spending in specific categories.
✅ Can provide actionable savings suggestions.
✅ Can suggest budget adjustments.
✅ Can offer relevant financial tips.
✅ Beautiful colored output.
